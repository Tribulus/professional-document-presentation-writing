# Moderate-density layouts

- **Chart and interpretation:** Give the chart the main viewing area. Group formulas, key results and interpretation beside it. Keep assumptions that affect the conclusion close to the result.
- **Process or network:** Use labeled nodes, direction and a legend to clarify relationships. Reserve a distinct area for the mechanisms or evidence the audience should take away.
- **Structured comparison:** Align categories, characteristics, implications and options in repeated rows or columns. Keep relevant classification conditions near the comparison.

Use spacing to separate information groups and type hierarchy to distinguish titles, labels, key results and sources. Adapt the styling and level of detail to the project.
